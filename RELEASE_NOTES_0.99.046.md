## Local co-op, languages and extra modes

- **Local 2-player co-op:** the second player controls Vaelin, Auralis' brother. More enemies show up and bosses are tougher.
- **Subtitles in 8 languages:** Portuguese, English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Korean and Japanese, with each country's flag in the picker.
- **Language screen** the first time you open the game: pick voice and subtitles before you start (changeable in Options).
- **Boss voices** in Portuguese and English.
- **Extra modes:** Boss Arena, the hidden final boss The Pale Curse and the Daily Challenge.
- Training dummy to learn the parry, a Continue screen with a summary of your game and an art gallery in the pause menu.
- Game page with trailer, gallery and the list of all 61 achievements.

Play in the browser: https://auralisgame.vercel.app/ · Windows: `releases/AURALIS-Setup.exe` (SHA-256 in `releases/SHA256.txt`).
The installer is not digitally signed yet: Windows may show the blue warning (More info → Run anyway).
